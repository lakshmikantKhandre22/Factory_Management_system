          Production Standard Schema
        


        
        

          

            
★ What's New & Updated

            
    
              
  • Supplier–Raw Material (1:N): One supplier can supply multiple raw materials.
    • 
              
  • Dedicated PRODUCT Entity: Finished goods catalog separated from orders.
    • 
              
  • ORDER_MATERIAL Weak Entity: Composite primary key (order_id, material_id) tracking materials used per batch.
    • 
              
  • Machine Multi-Assignment: Production orders map to specific machines with runtime status.
    • 
              
  • Employee Participation ("Works On"): Tracks multiple employees and roles assigned to each production order.
    • 
              
  • STORAGE Entity: Warehouse bays and silos with max capacity & stock utilization.
    • 
              
  • MAINTENANCE Entity: Full breakdown and service schedule tracking with costs and next due dates.
    • 
              
  • Triggers & Audit Logging: Automatic stock deduction upon allocation and automated stock_audit logging.
    • 
            

          

          

            
✓ Why This Architecture Excels

            
    
              
  • True 3rd Normal Form (3NF): Eliminates redundancy and update anomalies.
    • 
              
  • Enforced Referential Integrity: PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON prevents orphan records.
    • 
              
  • Comprehensive Cost Accounting: Computes material costs, machine service expenses, and department payroll vs budget.
    • 
              
  • Audit Compliance: Historic quantity changes are immutable in stock_audit.
    •
